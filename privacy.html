Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 August 2026

Short version: Apex keeps your training data on your iPhone. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no trackers.

Who this is from

Apex ("the app") is provided by Zeyuan Xue (individual developer) ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to information when you use the app. If anything here is unclear, email evanxue@rplbb.com and we will answer plainly.

The short version

  • You do not create an account. We do not know who you are.
  • Everything you log — workouts, sets, body weight, check-ins, notes — is stored in a database on your device.
  • We operate no servers that receive your training data. We cannot read it, and we could not hand it over if asked.
  • There is no analytics SDK, no crash reporter, no advertising, and no third-party tracking of any kind.
  • The only data that leaves your device is a purchase receipt, and only when you subscribe or restore a purchase.

What Apex stores on your device

All of the following lives in a local database inside the app's private storage, which iOS keeps isolated from other apps:

  • Profile — training goal, experience level, days per week, available equipment, unit preference, and the body stats you enter (sex, body weight, and optionally height and birth year).
  • Training — planned and completed workouts, exercises, sets, reps, weights, RPE, and any notes you write.
  • Strength records — your entered or estimated one-rep maxes and their history.
  • Daily check-ins — energy, hours slept, sleep quality, muscle soreness, and optional notes.
  • Derived training data — the fatigue events and readiness scores Apex computes from the above.
  • App settings — reminder time, theme, and similar preferences.

None of this is transmitted anywhere. It is included in your device backup if you have iCloud Backup or encrypted local backups turned on; that backup is governed by Apple's terms, not ours.

Health data

Apex does not connect to Apple Health. It neither reads from nor writes to HealthKit. The body and training information you enter is typed into Apex directly and stays in Apex.

Purchases

Apex is a paid app with a free trial. Subscriptions are sold by Apple through the App Store and managed for us by RevenueCat, Inc., which validates App Store receipts so the app knows whether your subscription is active.

WhoWhat they receiveWhy
AppleYour payment details, Apple ID and purchase historyTo take payment and manage the subscription. We never see your payment details.
RevenueCatThe App Store receipt, an anonymous app-generated user ID, and basic device/app information such as iOS version and app versionTo verify the receipt and tell the app whether your subscription is active.

The identifier RevenueCat uses is generated by the app and is not linked to your name, email or Apple ID. No training data, body data or check-in data is ever sent to RevenueCat or to anyone else. RevenueCat's own privacy policy is at revenuecat.com/privacy.

Notifications

Apex can send you two kinds of local notification: a rest-timer alert during a workout, and an optional morning check-in reminder. These are scheduled on your device by iOS. They are not push notifications, nothing is sent through a server, and Apex has no ability to message you remotely. You can turn them off in the app or in iOS Settings.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect analytics or usage statistics.
  • We do not use advertising, ad IDs, or the App Tracking Transparency framework — because we have nothing to track.
  • We do not sell, rent or share personal information. There is nothing to sell.
  • We do not build profiles or make automated decisions about you beyond the training suggestions shown inside the app.

Your control over your data

  • Export — Settings → Export data produces a complete JSON file of everything Apex stores, which you can share anywhere you like.
  • Delete — Settings → Delete all data erases the local database permanently. Deleting the app from your iPhone also removes it.
  • Purchase records — to have your RevenueCat records deleted, email us and we will pass the request on. Note that Apple keeps its own purchase records under its own policy.

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is generally nothing for us to produce, correct or erase in response to a GDPR or CCPA request. If you are in a region granting those rights, you may still contact us and we will help with anything held by RevenueCat.

Children

Apex is a strength-training app intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has used the app in a way that concerns you, contact us.

Security

Your data is protected by iOS app sandboxing and your device passcode or biometric lock. Network requests the app makes — only for purchases — use HTTPS. Because your training data never leaves the device, there is no server of ours that could be breached.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will update the date at the top and note the change in the app's release notes. Continuing to use Apex after a change means you accept the updated policy.
